Season pass

Rank

Each new division in the season gives a reward the first time you reach it. A new rank gives a pack.

The ranksBronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum and Diamond each have three divisions: III, II and I. Each division is 100 rank points (RP). Above Diamond I is Legend, where the points just keep going.
Points per matchWin +25, draw +5, loss -18. A stronger opponent gives up to 10 extra, a weaker one a little less. In Bronze you only lose half, in Silver three quarters.
Win streakTwo wins in a row give +5 extra, three give +10, four or more +15. A win by 3 goals or more gives +3.
ShieldsWhen you reach a new rank, you get 3 shields. If a loss would send you down to the rank below, a shield is used instead and you stay.
Which matches countFind opponent gives full points. Matches with friends via code give half points. 7 v 7 and 11 v 11 each have their own rank.
The seasonEach month is a season. When it ends, you get coins for your best rank (Gold and up also gives a pack), and you start one rank lower.

      How to play

      JOYSTICKPut your thumb anywhere on the left side and steer. Pull far out to sprint.
      SHOOTWith the ball. Hold for a harder shot. Tap twice quickly for a lob over the keeper.
      PASSWith the ball. Point the joystick at the teammate you want to pass to. If he is ahead of you, the ball is played into his run. Hold for a through ball in behind the defenders (blue ring): the longer you hold, the further ahead of him it goes.
      LOBWith the ball. A high ball over the others to the teammate you point at; hold to reach a man further away. Out wide near their box it is a cross.
      TACKLEWithout the ball. A sliding tackle aimed at the ball. From behind, it is a free kick.
      OFFSIDEIn 5, 7 and 11 a side. If the receiver is behind the defence when the ball is played, the opponents get a free kick.
      HEADERWhen the ball comes through the air, the player heads it automatically. Near goal it goes towards goal.
      FREE KICKNear goal the opponents set up a wall. A shot from the free kick lifts over the wall. If they foul another man while your team has the ball, the referee plays advantage and the game goes on.
      CARDSTackle from behind or hit the man far from the ball and it is a free kick, in the box a penalty. A hard tackle from behind or another foul gives a yellow card. A second yellow, or stopping a free man on his way to goal, gives a red card and time off.
      GOALKEEPERThe keeper dives for shots himself. When he catches the ball, you control him: hold KICK for a long kick (the line shows where it lands), or tap THROW to a teammate. If you are the keeper in the box yourself, you dive with DIVE.
      PENALTIESAim with the joystick and tap SHOOT. As keeper the joystick decides which way you dive.
      SWITCHWithout the ball. Take the defender best placed between the ball and your goal (a faint ring shows who). Tap again quickly for the next one.
      ADDED TIMEEach half gets time added for free kicks, goals and injuries. If it is level after full time, extra time is played before penalties (can be turned off).
      SUBSTITUTIONSTired and injured players are subbed off at half-time and late in the match. Against the computer you can sub yourself in the pause menu.
      CAMERAPick Normal, Close or Wide in Settings. On goals, cards, free kicks and corners the camera moves in close with film bars (Film clips can be turned off).
      AIM LINEWhile you hold SHOOT, and at free kicks and corners, a dotted line shows where the ball goes. Gold means it is on target. Can be turned off in Settings.
      ADVANCEDPick Buttons: Advanced in Settings. Then you only sprint with the SPRINT button, with the ball FEINT gives a quick sidestep past the defender, and without the ball you hold PRESS to close down the man on the ball.
